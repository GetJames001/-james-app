James Privacy Policy

Internal review draft — not effective or published. This page is a design prototype. Its disclosures require factual verification before publication.

About James

James is a personal assistant being developed to help an authorized user organize schedules, messages, tasks and daily activities. The available integrations may change.

Google Calendar

The current integration requests read-only Google Calendar access to retrieve calendar information for display and scheduling awareness. James uses a server-side OAuth refresh token to request access tokens. The current integration does not request Calendar write permission.

Other information and services

James has mail- and task-related functionality. Before this policy becomes effective, the active providers, information collected, processing purposes, AI providers, subprocessors and disclosures must be documented accurately.

Security

The current application uses authenticated sessions and server-side token handling. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention, deletion and user choices

Retention periods and deletion procedures are being verified. Users may revoke Google authorization through their Google Account. The application-specific deletion process will be documented before publication.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@getjames.ai.

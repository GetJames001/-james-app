James Guide

Daily Briefing

Start here for the weather, your next appointment and today’s perspective. Tap a tile to open Mail, Wardrobe or your trips. Choose Briefing in the sidebar to return.

Appointments

Choose a date to see its appointments. Tap a meeting for details and any joining link. The Briefing shows drive estimates for appointments with a destination.

Mail

Open Mail to read recent messages, compose an email or return to saved drafts. Refresh inbox checks Outlook for changes, including messages you deleted there.

Mail settings & review details

Get Android alerts for newly received actionable mail and hotel confirmations. Alerts stay on until you stop them.

Connect or reconnect Gmail

Use this only if Gmail needs connecting. Existing Calendar access is preserved.

Saved review history & coverage

See which mail James checked and when.

Needs Attention

Open To Do to review mail James flagged. Each item shows why it surfaced. Choose Draft a task to create a next step or Mark handled to dismiss the suggestion. The original email stays in its folder.

Tasks

Open To Do and choose Tasks to add something you want to do. Check it off when you finish. For a timed reminder while James is open, use the reminder controls below.

Reminders
Travel

Open Travel from the sidebar or the Briefing tile. Choose an upcoming trip to see its dates, stops and itinerary.

Workbench and Files

Open Workbench to return to a project. Open Files to find an upload or add a file for James to use.

Customer Information

Use the Customer Vault to find a company or import a customer file. Add a company to today’s list, then open it in Customer Activity to record a visit, call or follow-up.

Wardrobe

Choose Wardrobe, then Open Wardrobe. Your closet, inspiration and saved looks open in a separate tab.

Lifestyle

Choose a photograph to open its section. Cooking & Recipes has a recipe book and a live discovery feed. Personal Development brings reading and career files together. Finance shows sample accounts and card payments. Open Happening today for business stories or choose Investments to view the portfolio area.

James and voice

Choose James in the sidebar to type a question, or tap the James orb to speak. Open Settings and choose Voice to change your voice preferences.

Settings

Open Settings at the top of James for Connections, Voice, your entrance scene, the light or dark theme and Wall display.
